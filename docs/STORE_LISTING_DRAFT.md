## Suggested full description

AURUM is a cryptocurrency market-analysis and decision-support application designed to help you explore market context with a clear, premium mobile experience.

- Follow cryptocurrency market prices, 24-hour movement, volume and market context.
- Explore asset charts, technical indicators and multi-timeframe market observations.
- Review structured AURUM intelligence with supporting and conflicting factors, scenarios, risk context and invalidation conditions.
- Save markets to a personal watchlist and create price alerts.
- Review explainable analytical signal records and notifications.
- Manage account, notification and application preferences.

AURUM provides analysis and informational tools only. Market information can be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate. Technical indicators, intelligence summaries and signals are not financial advice and do not guarantee profits, returns, accuracy or future price movements. Cryptocurrency markets are volatile, and you remain responsible for your own decisions.
